# Community conduct

Keep issues, pull requests and project discussions respectful, constructive and
focused on evidence. Welcome participants with different experience and viewpoints.
Critique technical proposals without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption and disclosure
of another person's confidential information are unacceptable.

Repository administrators may edit/remove abusive content or restrict participation
within their actual hosting permissions. Proposed enforcement should be proportionate:
clarification or warning for correctable conduct, restrictions for repeated or
serious harm, and a record that protects affected people's privacy. Administrators
with a conflict should recuse themselves where another authorized administrator is
available. A community policy does not establish an enforcement team or private
reporting inbox.

For public moderation needs, use the platform's report controls or ask repository
administrators to review the relevant public thread. Do not post sensitive conduct
reports or identifying details publicly. A dedicated confidential contact and an
appeal/escalation owner remain to be appointed; no private response-time promise
is made. Use the hosting platform's confidential reporting mechanisms where
available. Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public conduct
thread.

General technical support uses repository issues with a minimal reproduction and
public environment details, excluding secrets and customer data. Support is
best-effort for the current PoC; no custody, issuer, financial-recovery or service
availability obligation is created by participation. See
[contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [maintenance policy](docs/maintenance.md).
